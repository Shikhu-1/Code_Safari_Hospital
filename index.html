Welcome to Code Safari

Healthcare Built Around You

At Code Safari Hospital, we believe that healthcare should be accessible, respectful and centred on each patient's needs. Every person deserves to be listened to, informed and treated with dignity.

Our hospital brings together a patient-focused approach, medical expertise and a commitment to continuous improvement to support healthier individuals and communities.

  • Patient-centred care and clear communication
  • A welcoming environment for individuals and families
  • A focus on prevention, wellbeing and health education
  • Respect for patient privacy and dignity
